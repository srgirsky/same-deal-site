Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

Same Deal ("we", "us") is a head-to-head skill-game app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we don't do with it.

What we collect

  • A device identifier we generate when you first open the app, so your credits and match history can be found again. It is random and is not your Apple advertising identifier.
  • Gameplay data: the moves you make in each match, your scores, ratings, credit balance, and match history. We replay your moves on our server to verify scores; this is how we keep matches fair.
  • Your display name, which you choose and which other players can see on leaderboards and match results.
  • If you sign in with Apple: the stable identifier Apple provides and, if you share it, your name and email address. We use them only to link your account across devices. You can use Apple's "Hide My Email" option.
  • If you enable notifications: a push token so we can tell you when a match you're waiting on has been decided.
  • Technical logs such as request timestamps and error reports, kept briefly to keep the service running.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers, and the app contains no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect your precise location. (When cash play launches, state-level location checks will be required by law; we will update this policy first.)
  • We do not collect payment details. Credits in the current version are free, have no cash value, and cannot be purchased or withdrawn.

How long we keep it

Match records, including move logs, are kept so results can be audited and disputes resolved. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time; we complete deletion within 30 days.

Children

Same Deal is intended for players 18 and older and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Change your display name in Profile.
  • Turn notifications off in iOS Settings.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your data by emailing seth@wordontheblock.biz.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes and the app will show the update before you play.
