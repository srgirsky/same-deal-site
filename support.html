How do matches work?

Pick a game and stakes. You and your opponent get the exact same deal, course, or seed. Whoever scores higher wins the pool minus the published fee. If nobody is waiting, you play first and the next player who picks the same game and stakes gets 24 hours to answer your score; if no one does, your entry is refunded.

Are the credits real money?

No. Credits in this version are free, have no cash value, and can't be bought or withdrawn. You get 1,000 a day automatically, and if you drop under 100 you can be staked a few times a day.

Is the computer opponent a bot pretending to be a person?

No. Computer opponents are always labelled "Computer" with their difficulty. Anyone labelled with a name is a real player.

My match is stuck on "waiting for opponent"

Your score is locked in. The match settles when someone plays the same deal, or after 24 hours with a refund. Pull down on the Play screen to refresh.

Delete my account

Email us from the address you'd like us to reply to, with your display name. We delete the account and its data within 30 days.
