How do matches work?

Pick a game and a credit entry level. You and your opponent get the exact same deal, course, or seed. Whoever scores higher wins the full credit pool. Same Deal takes no cut, and ties return each player’s entry. If nobody is waiting, you play first and the next player who picks the same game and entry level gets 24 hours to answer your score; if no one does, your entry is refunded.

Are the credits real money?

No. Credits are free, have no cash value, and cannot be purchased, transferred between accounts, or redeemed for money or prizes. You start with 1,000, receive 1,000 more each day you return, and can claim a 300-credit refill up to three times a day when your balance is below 100. Practice is always free.

Is the computer opponent a bot pretending to be a person?

No. Computer opponents are always labelled "Computer" with their difficulty. Online player matches are against real people. Offline player scores are simulated and the app identifies offline mode; choose Computer to play a computer opponent on your device.

My match is stuck on "waiting for opponent"

Your score is locked in. The match settles when someone plays the same deal, or after 24 hours with a refund. Pull down on the Play screen to refresh.

Delete my account

While connected, open Profile → Delete account and confirm. For help or a data request, email us with your display name; we respond to deletion requests within 30 days.
