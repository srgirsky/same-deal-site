Same Deal

The house keeps 6 cents. You keep the rest.

Head-to-head skill games. Both players get the exact same deal. Every score is verified by replaying your moves. Every match shows the receipt.

Join the free beta How it works

Fair by construction

Most cash-game apps keep 10–30% of every pool and spend it buying players. We keep only what it costs to run the service, and we publish the numbers.

Same deal, same clock

Your opponent plays the identical shuffle, course, or seed. Whoever plays it better wins. No luck of the draw.

Verified, not trusted

Your phone sends the moves, not the score. The server replays them and computes the result itself.

No bots pretending to be people

Computer opponents exist, at three difficulties, and they are always labelled. Real opponents are real.

Free credits now, cash later

The beta runs on free credits with no cash value while we prove the games are fair and fun. Cash play comes state by state, at cost.

The receipt on a 100-credit match

Two players put in 100 each. This is where every credit goes. When cash launches, the same six percent applies and the rate drops if it ever exceeds our costs.
